# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS in a single file, published on GitHub Pages (lucasyleodigital.github.io). Confirmed by the user.

## Users

Board members (juntas directivas) of small non-profit associations in Gavà and nearby towns: neighbourhood, cultural, sports, social and educational entities, run by volunteers with little time. Most have no website of their own or an outdated one. They open this page from a cold outreach email sent by Lucas y Leo Digital, often on a phone.

## Product Purpose

A demonstration website for a fictional association. It shows what a website for their entity could look like so they think "ours could look like this" and reply to the email. Success is a reply asking for a call or a quote.

## Positioning

Lucas y Leo Digital is a small, close-to-the-client web studio from Gavà that builds websites for entities at a reduced promotional price and stays with them after delivery (direct contact, no tickets). The demo can be personalised with the entity's own name via the `?entidad=` URL parameter.

## Operating Context

- Linked from a 3-email outreach sequence (Catalan or Spanish) sent to entities from the Gavà municipal register of associations.
- Personalised link: `?entidad=<name>&lang=ca|es`.
- Visitors decide quickly; the reply happens in their email client, not on the page.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Bilingual Catalan (default) and Spanish, switchable.
- Sections an entity typically needs: who we are, activities/calendar, become a member / volunteer / donate, news, gallery, transparency documents, contact with map.
- Forms are demonstrations only: nothing is sent or stored, and the page says so.
- Page must be `noindex` and clearly marked as a demo by Lucas y Leo Digital.
- Open decision: final promotional price and number of slots are not shown on the page.

## Brand Commitments

- No emojis anywhere in the UI; use icon-library glyphs instead.
- Studio name written "Lucas y Leo Digital"; main site https://lucasyleodigital.com.

## Evidence on Hand

- No testimonials, client logos, metrics or photos of real entities. None may be invented.
- All texts, dates, addresses and images on the demo are illustrative and must be labelled as examples.

## Product Principles

1. The visitor must recognise their own entity in it within seconds.
2. Honest demo: everything fictional is visibly an example.
3. Make the next step obvious: reply to the email or contact the studio.
4. Works perfectly on a phone and loads instantly.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Audience includes older volunteers: readable type sizes, strong contrast, keyboard access, reduced-motion support.
